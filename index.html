Questions

Good to know

Do my staff need to create accounts or install an app?

No. Staff open the property's kiosk link or scan the QR code, tap their name and enter a PIN. The PIN comes from their staff record, so nothing needs to be created or remembered.

Does it work offline?

The kiosk needs an internet connection, so we don't promise offline use.

Can I use the paper checklists I already have?

Yes. You can photograph a paper form to generate the checklist, then review it before it goes live.

How do alerts reach my team?

End-of-shift reports go out by email, and urgent maintenance alerts and shift reports can go over WhatsApp. WhatsApp delivery depends on approved message templates and a configured business number, so we'll confirm that during onboarding.

Can I add more properties later?

Yes. Companies hold properties, with a property switcher everywhere, and master templates copy your standards into each new site.
